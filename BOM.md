# Bill of materials

Shopping list for the [design](DESIGN.md): refrigerator, flat screen, modem and router, LED lights, and an airway-clearance device. US retail ranges, October 2026, parts only, before tax and shipping. The battery and the inverter set the total. The phlegm-clearance device changes the surge the inverter must cover. It does not change the parts below, because a 1,000 W inverter and a 24 V 200 Ah battery still cover the day.

The parts total omits the series jumper and the monitor. A listed 1,000 W 24 V pure-sine inverter, such as a Samlex unit, sits near the top of the inverter range, around $600. A 24 V 200 Ah LiFePO4 pack is about $1,050–$2,160.

Fuse and cable sizes are 125% of the inverter’s full DC input and the charger current. The inverter manual overrides them.
