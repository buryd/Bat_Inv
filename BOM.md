The parts total omits the series jumper and the monitor. A listed 1,000 W 24 V pure-sine inverter, such as a Samlex unit, sits near the top of the inverter range, around $600. A UL 1973 24 V 150 Ah pack, such as SOK, lists near $1,150, inside the battery range.

Fuse and cable sizes are 125% of the inverter’s full DC input and the charger current. The inverter manual overrides them.
