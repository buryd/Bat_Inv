# Battery backup

120 V pure-sine backup for a refrigerator, a flat screen, internet gear, LED lights, and an airway-clearance device that clears phlegm (a chest vest or cough-assist machine).

- [Design](DESIGN.md) — loads, equipment, and wiring
- [Schematic](backup-schematic.canvas.tsx) — battery, fuses, inverter, charger, and outlets
- [Bill of materials](BOM.md) — parts and October 2026 US price ranges

Parts for this system are about **$1,721–$3,620** before tax and shipping. One 24 V 200 Ah LiFePO4 battery and a 1,000 W inverter make up most of that.
